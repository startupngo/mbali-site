Questions producers ask

Do I have to repackage or change my product?

No. Export it exactly as you sell it at home. If the label needs UK details we add a compliant over-label here — your brand stays your brand.

Who pays for freight and duty?

We buy from you ex-works or FOB and pay the freight. Goods from Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda enter the UK duty-free under the Developing Countries Trading Scheme, and Kenyan goods under the UK–Kenya agreement, so there is no duty to argue over.

Is it exclusive?

We ask for UK exclusivity on the lines we take, for a fixed period, in exchange for real sales targets you can hold us to.

How big does a first order need to be?

Small. Our first orders are typically one pallet or an air-freight consignment, so we can prove sell-through before scaling up.
